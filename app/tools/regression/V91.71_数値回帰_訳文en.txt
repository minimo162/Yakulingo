[Operating Results]
Consolidated results for the consolidated cumulative first quarter were net sales of 12,857 oku, an increase of 1,859 oku, or 16.9%, year on year; operating profit of 328 oku, compared with a loss of 461 oku in the same period of the previous year; and ordinary profit of 428 oku, compared with a loss of 343 oku in the same period of the previous year. Quarterly net profit attributable to owners of the parent was 296 oku, compared with a loss of 421 oku in the same period of the previous year, due in part to income taxes of 99 oku.

(２) Overview of Financial Position and Cash Flows for the Consolidated Cumulative First Quarter
[Assets, Liabilities and Net Assets]
Total assets at the end of the consolidated first quarter were 44,343 oku, a decrease of 452 oku from the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, and total liabilities were 24,967 oku, a decrease of 579 oku from the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year. Net assets were 19,376 oku, an increase of 127 oku from the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, due in part to quarterly net profit attributable to owners of the parent of 296 oku. The shareholders’ equity ratio was 43.3%, an increase of 0.7 percentage points from the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, or 44.0% after taking into account the equity nature of subordinated loans.
[Cash Flows]
At the end of the consolidated first quarter, cash and cash equivalents were 12,148 oku, a decrease of 784 oku from the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, and interest-bearing debt was 8,429 oku, a decrease of 72 oku. As a result, the Company had a net cash position of 3,719 oku.
The status of each cash flow for the consolidated cumulative first quarter was as follows.
Cash Flows from Operating Activities
Cash flows from operating activities decreased by 406 oku, compared with a decrease of 1,411 oku in the same period of the previous year, due to factors including an increase in inventories, against quarterly net profit before income taxes of 399 oku.

(Financing through a New Subordinated Loan and Early Repayment of Existing Subordinated Loans)
On July 21, 2026, the Company conducted financing through a new subordinated loan (hereinafter, the "Subordinated Loan") and early repayment of existing subordinated loans (hereinafter, the "Existing Subordinated Loans"). The details are as follows.
(１) Overview of the Subordinated Loan
Amount borrowed 700 oku
Use of funds Applied to repayment of the Existing Subordinated Loans
Execution date July 21, 2026
Repayment date
July 21, 2086
However, all or part of the principal may be repaid early on each interest payment date on or after July 21, 2031, or if certain other events occur.
Refinancing restriction clause
When making early repayment of the Subordinated Loan, the Company intends to refinance within 12 months before the early repayment date through financing that has been recognized by a credit rating agency as having equity characteristics equal to or greater than those of the Subordinated Loan. However, for early repayment on or after July 21, 2031, if certain financial criteria are met, the Company may elect not to refinance through financing that has been recognized by a credit rating agency as having equity characteristics equal to or greater than those of the Subordinated Loan.
Clause regarding interest payments Except when dividends are paid on common shares or in similar circumstances, the Company may, at its discretion, defer all or part of the interest payments on the Subordinated Loan.
Subordination clause
If liquidation, bankruptcy, corporate reorganization, civil rehabilitation, or equivalent proceedings not governed by Japanese law are commenced and remain ongoing with respect to the Company, the right to demand payment of claims under this agreement shall become effective subject to the condition precedent that all claims, other than claims with subordination clauses or other provisions substantially similar to those of the Subordinated Loan, have been paid in full or otherwise fully satisfied.
The provisions of this agreement shall not be amended in any manner that is detrimental to creditors other than creditors of subordinated claims.
